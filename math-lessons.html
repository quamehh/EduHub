Fractions & Shapes

Understanding Basic Fractions

A fraction represents a part of a whole. When an object or a shape is split into completely equal sizes, we express those pieces using fractions.

📐 Core Mathematical Terms:

  • Numerator: The number on top. It shows how many parts you are talking about.
  • Denominator: The number on the bottom. It shows the total number of equal pieces the whole object was split into.

For example, if you slice a circle into 4 equal quarters and color in 1 slice, the fraction of the colored part is written as $\frac{1}{4}$.
