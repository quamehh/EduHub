Energy & Environment

Our Primary Sources of Energy

Energy is defined simply as the ability to do work. In our environment, living things depend heavily on natural forces, and the primary source of heat and light energy for the Earth is the Sun.

📝 Quick Vocabulary Summary:

  • Solar Energy: Light and heat radiant energy that comes directly from the Sun.
  • Photosynthesis: The natural process plants use to manufacture their own food using sunlight.

Without solar energy, plants would not be able to grow, which would make life impossible for animals and humans who rely on plants for oxygen and nourishment.
